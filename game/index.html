A DUEL OF CARDS

EMBER
COURT

Two alchemists. One court. Thirty life each, and a hand full of fire, tide, thorn and stone.

Play cards — click a card or press 1–7, then Enter. Each costs mana.
Match suits — play two cards of the same suit in a row and the second one surges: +2 power and you draw a card.
Read Rook — his intent is shown before he acts. Guard when a big strike is coming.
Win — bring Rook to 0 life. Lose yours and the court is his.

Mouse + keyboard · no network · your deck is shuffled fresh every duel

Victory

How to play

Goal — reduce Rook to 0 life before he reduces you to 0.
Mana — you gain +1 mana each turn (max 10). Unspent mana is lost.
Strike — deals its power as damage. Guard — blocks damage until your next turn. Mend — heals you. Surge — damage plus a bonus when it follows the same suit.
Suits — Ember (red flame), Tide (blue droplet), Thorn (green leaf), Stone (grey diamond). Colour and shape tell them apart.
Keys — 1–7 select a card, Enter plays it, E ends your turn, R starts a new duel.
